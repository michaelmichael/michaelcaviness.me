Selected work

Automated chemical dosing

Benziger Winery approached us about an existing chemical dosing system that was unreliable and expensive to maintain.

Working with their team, I developed a replacement based on a peristaltic pump, eliminating two particularly troublesome components: the flow meter and electronic valve.

The project grew to include timed and volumetric dosing, wireless operation, and a touchscreen interface. I worked with a PLC programmer on the original controls, then learned the programming environment myself to troubleshoot and extend the system.

Pneumatic bottle rinsers

A customer at Breckenridge Distillery asked us to modify an existing bottle rinser to recirculate spirits instead of water.

That request exposed a broader problem: distilleries needed bottle-rinsing equipment suitable for handling flammable liquids, without the electrical components or grounding concerns of conventional designs.

I knew almost nothing about pneumatic controls, so I started reading the Clippard catalog. Eventually, I developed a completely pneumatic design using commercially available components.

The resulting product family has sold approximately 235 units across its primary configurations.

E-commerce and business systems

When I started developing TCW's online store, the available off-the-shelf platforms didn't offer the flexibility I wanted for configuring and selling specialized industrial equipment.

Rather than build something entirely bespoke, I started with open-source software, eventually adopting Spree and Solidus, which is still in use to this day.

Over the years, I've worked on the storefront, backend integrations, and business systems, including connecting our commerce platform with Odoo for order processing, inventory, and fulfillment.

Background

Since 2014, I've worked at TCW Equipment, a small company supplying equipment to wineries, breweries, and distilleries.

My work there has ranged from product development and manufacturing to software, business systems, customer support, and the ordinary challenges of running a business.

Before TCW, I worked at Audible, where I taught myself Ruby to keep audiobook metadata flowing to iTunes after the existing publishing workflow was discontinued, and to automate merchandising/editorial workflows.

It started as a collection of scripts, and ended up a practical part of our publishing operations.
